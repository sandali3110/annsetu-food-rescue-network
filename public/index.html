    How a rescue works

    1. A kitchen posts extra food

      They add what it is, how many people it can feed and until when it stays safe to eat.

    2. A rescuer claims it

      NGOs and volunteers see food near them, sorted by distance or by how soon it expires, and claim it with an arrival time.

    3. They swap a pickup code

      The rescuer shows a 4-digit code. The kitchen enters it to confirm they handed the food to the right person.

    4. Food reaches people

      The rescuer records how many people were fed, and the impact shows up on this page.
